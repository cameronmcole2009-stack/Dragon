Dragons Drive Safe is a student-run campaign at Carroll High School in Southlake, Texas. We use booths, lunch tables and funny videos to get teens to put the phone down, slow down, and buckle up.

Car crashes are a leading killer of American teens.

8

teens die in car crashes in the U.S. every day, on average.

3×

Drivers aged 16 to 19 are nearly three times as likely to be in a fatal crash as drivers 20 and older.

Most

of these crashes come down to things we can change: phones, speed, music and showing off for friends.

Sources: CDC, NHTSA, WHO.

We asked 100+ Carroll students

Our own survey showed the risks are right here in our parking lot.

  • regularly drive over the speed limit
    67%
  • say loud music is their biggest distraction
    44%
  • put having fun ahead of driving safely
    20%+

Going just 10 mph over the limit can double your risk of a crash.

We go where teens already are.

Lectures don't work on teenagers. Talking to them at lunch, at the game and on their feed does.

Booths with Southlake PD

We set up at community events around Southlake, often with Southlake Police officers right next to us. They helped design our posters and donated materials for our tables.

  • 5,000+ people reached in person
  • 200+ posters and pamphlets

Lunch tables and game days

At school we run tables at lunch and at games with discount cards, a gift basket raffle, and actual conversations about driving. Every dollar goes to Mothers Against Drunk Driving.

  • 150+ students at our first lunch tables
  • Schoolwide announcement to 1,200+

Chalk in the parking lot

30+ safety messages chalked in the student lot, where 1,200+ people walk past every day.

Funny videos, not scary ones

Short skits on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Teens share a joke way faster than a warning.

Posters with a QR code

Posters and pamphlets around campus link straight to our survey so students can weigh in.
