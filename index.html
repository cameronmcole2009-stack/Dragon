Crashes are a leading killer of teens.

Most of them come down to things we can change.

8

teens die in car crashes in the U.S. every day, on average.

3×

Drivers 16 to 19 are nearly three times as likely to be in a fatal crash as drivers 20 and older.

2×

Going just 10 mph over the limit can double your risk of a crash.

We asked 100+ Carroll students.

The risks are right here in our own parking lot.

  • 67%
    regularly drive over the speed limit
  • 44%
    say loud music is their biggest distraction
  • 20%+
    put having fun ahead of driving safely

Sources: CDC, NHTSA, WHO, and our own student survey.
